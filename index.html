❓ How to Play

🎲 Roll the dice and select a pawn that can move by the rolled number.

🎯 4 and 8 give an extra roll. They occur less often than the other numbers.

⚔️ Capture an opponent pawn to get an extra roll.

⭐ Safe cells cannot be captured.

🏠 Reach HOME to get an extra roll, unless all four of your pawns have already reached HOME.

🏆 Move all four pawns to HOME to finish.

🎤 Voice chat works in online games.
