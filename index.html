How to Play

🎲 Roll the dice on your turn.

Dice values are: 1, 2, 3, 4 and 8.

A 4 or 8 gives an extra roll after you complete the move.

Capturing an opponent gives an extra roll.

Reaching HOME gives an extra roll, unless that move finishes all four pawns.

Before your first capture, your pawn can move up to step 15.

After you capture, your pawn can continue up to HOME at step 24.

Tap a glowing pawn to move it.
