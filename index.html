How to Play

Roll the dice on your turn. Dice values are 1, 2, 3, 4 and 8. A 4 or 8 gives an extra roll after the move. Capture an opponent for an extra roll. Reaching HOME gives an extra roll unless you have finished all four pawns. Before your first capture, a pawn can reach step 15; after you capture, it can reach step 24. Tap a glowing pawn to move it.
