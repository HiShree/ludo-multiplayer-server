❓ How to Play

🎲 Roll the dice to get a number.

🔢 If you have more than one available number, tap the number you want to use first.

🔄 Rolling 4 or 8 gives you another chance to roll before moving.

⚡ Capturing an opponent gives an extra roll.

🏠 Reaching HOME gives an extra roll unless you have already finished the game.

🏆 Move all four pawns to HOME to finish.

🛡️ Safe cells cannot be used to capture opponents.
