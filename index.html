❓ How to Play

🎲 Roll the dice when it is your turn.

🔵 Select a movable pawn after rolling.

⭐ Rolling 4 or 8 gives you another roll.

🎯 Capturing an opponent gives another roll.

🏠 Move all four pawns to HOME to finish.

🔢 The dice displays the actual rolled number.
