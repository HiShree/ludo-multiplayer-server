How to Play

• Dice rolls are 1, 2, 3, 4 or 8.
• 4 or 8 gives an extra turn after moving.
• Before the first capture, pawns can move up to 15 steps.
• After a capture, pawns can move up to 24 steps.
• Reaching HOME gives an extra turn.
• Capturing gives an extra turn.
• All 4 pawns at HOME finishes the player.
