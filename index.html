How to Play

• Dice values are 1, 2, 3, 4 and 8. The dice uses the original weighted roll system.
• A 4 or 8 gives an extra roll after that move.
• Capturing an opponent gives an extra roll.
• Reaching HOME gives an extra roll unless all four pawns finish.
• Before your first capture, pawns can move only through step 15. After a capture, they can continue to HOME at step 24.
• Safe cells cannot capture pawns.
