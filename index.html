❓ How to Play

🎲 Roll the dice.

🔢 Dice can show 1, 2, 3, 4 or 8.

⭐ Rolling 4 or 8 gives you another roll before you have to move.

🎲 You can store multiple dice. For example: 4 + 8 + 2

👉 Tap the dice number you want to use first.

🟢 A glowing pawn can be selected for the currently selected dice number.

💥 Capturing an opponent gives an extra roll.

🏠 Reaching HOME gives an extra roll.

🏆 Move all four pawns HOME to finish.
