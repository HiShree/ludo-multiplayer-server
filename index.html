❓ How to Play

• Dice values are 1, 2, 3, 4 and 8.

• 4 and 8 are rare lucky numbers.

• A 4 or 8 gives an extra roll.

• Capturing an opponent gives an extra roll.

• Reaching HOME gives an extra roll unless all four pawns finish.

• Before your first capture, pawns can move only through step 15.

• After a capture, they can continue to HOME at step 24.

• Safe cells cannot capture pawns.
