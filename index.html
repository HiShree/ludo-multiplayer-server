How to Play

Movement: Follow the faint arrows on the board.

  • Tap your glowing dice to roll a 1, 2, 3, 4, or 8.
  • Roll a 4 or 8 to get an extra turn!
  • You can bank extra turns and keep rolling before moving your pawns!

Combat: Land on an opponent to capture them.

  • Colored start zones and the center cross (HOME) are safe.
  • Inner Shell: You MUST capture at least one opponent before entering Home!
