How To Play

🎲 Roll the dice when it is your turn.

Numbers are: 1, 2, 3, 4 and 8.

⭐ Rolling 4 or 8 gives an extra roll.

♟️ Tap a glowing pawn to move it.

💥 Capturing another pawn gives an extra turn.

🏠 Get all four pawns to HOME to finish.

Online games are controlled by the server to keep everyone synchronized.
