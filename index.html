How To Play

🎲 Tap the glowing dice to roll.

🎯 Roll values can be 1, 2, 3, 4 or 8.

⭐ Rolling 4 or 8 gives another roll.

👆 After rolling, tap a glowing pawn to move it.

🏠 A pawn reaching HOME gives an extra turn.

⚔️ Capturing another pawn gives an extra turn.
