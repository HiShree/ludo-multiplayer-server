❓ How to Play

🎲 Tap your dice when it is your turn.

1, 2 and 3 move a pawn by that many spaces.

4 and 8 give you another roll.

Capturing an opponent gives another roll.

Each pawn must reach HOME exactly.

Move all four pawns to HOME to win.

In Offline 2 Player mode, both players use the same phone.
